[08] FAQ

Before your first rep.

What are your timings?

We're open 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, all seven days. Early risers and late finishers are both covered.

How much does a membership cost?

Listed prices: Day Pass ₹200, Monthly ₹2,500, Half-Yearly ₹8,000 and Annual ₹10,000. Longer plans work out far cheaper per day. Ask the front desk about current offers.

I've never been to a gym. Is Ace right for me?

Yes. Members call Ace an ideal gym for beginners because trainers stay on the floor to guide form, set up equipment and answer questions. Start with a day pass if you want to feel it out.

Do you offer classes as well as the gym floor?

Yes: CrossFit, functional training, Zumba, yoga, kickboxing and dedicated abs batches twice a week, plus personal training and nutrition consultation. Batch timings change through the year, so check with the desk.

Is Ace women-friendly?

Ace is a unisex club with CCTV-monitored floors, changing rooms and showers. Ask the front desk about batches that suit your schedule.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's ample free parking right at the gym.
